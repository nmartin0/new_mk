# BACKLOG

**The one list.** Everything open, in one place: the kernel, the
personality, the userland, the repository, and the open questions.

Ordering lives in `ROADMAP.md`, not here — this file says *what*, that
one says *when* and *why then*. Reasoning lives in `DECISIONS.md` (why a
thing was decided), `FINDINGS.md` and `FINDINGS2.md` (what was measured),
and `DISTRIBUTED.md` (the long-horizon design). Those files keep their
reasoning; they no longer keep lists.

**Before starting anything below, check the premise.** This list has
been wrong twice in ways that mattered: it claimed the system was
blocked on a libc for weeks after it was booting multi-user, and it sized
the vendor deviation at 432 files when the real figure is 16. A sentence
with no number in it is an estimate wearing the clothes of a finding
(RULES.md 1.12, 2.3).

**Status.** Items are open unless marked. `done` means measured as
working; `superseded` means the item was replaced and the replacement is
named; `answered` on a question means the answer is recorded in place.
